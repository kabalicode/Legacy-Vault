# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

LegacyVault is built with a **Privacy-First, Offline-Only** philosophy. We believe that your financial and family data should remain entirely under your control.

## 1. Data Collection
LegacyVault **does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers**. All information you enter into the application, including:
- Family member details (names, relationships, PAN numbers)
- Asset and account information (balances, account numbers, institution names)
- Nominee information
- Vault notes and physical location details

is stored **locally on your device only**.

## 2. Data Encryption
All sensitive data within the LegacyVault app is protected using industry-standard encryption:
- **Algorithm:** AES-256-GCM (Advanced Encryption Standard).
- **Key Derivation:** Keys are derived using PBKDF2-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations.
- **Access Control:** Data can only be decrypted using your device's authentication (Biometric, PIN, Pattern, or Password). 

## 3. Third-Party Access
Because LegacyVault operates entirely offline:
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
- **No Cloud Sync:** There is no automatic synchronization with Google Drive, iCloud, or any other cloud provider.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** Your data is never shared with, or sold to, any third parties.

## 4. Permissions
LegacyVault requests minimal permissions to function:
- **Biometric/Authentication:** Used only to trigger the system's own secure unlock prompt. The app never sees or stores your actual device credentials.
- **Storage/Media:** Used solely to allow you to export and import your own encrypted backup files (`.vault`) to your device's Downloads folder.

## 5. User Responsibility
- **Data Recovery:** Since we have no access to your data, we cannot recover your vault if you lose your device or forget your secondary App PIN (if enabled). 
- **Backups:** You are responsible for creating regular encrypted backups using the **Export** feature and storing them in a secure, separate location.
- **Transfer Passphrase:** Ensure your backup transfer passphrase is kept safe, as it is the only way to restore your data to a new device.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
If we update this application to include any features that change how data is handled (such as opt-in cloud sync), this policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Contact
As an offline-first application, LegacyVault does not have a backend. For support or inquiries, please reach out to BejugamCreations@gmail.com
